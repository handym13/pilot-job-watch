Paid time building

1947
The DHC-2 and DHC-3
De Havilland Canada designed the Beaver and the Otter by asking bush pilots what they actually needed. The answer was full-span flaps, doors that fit a fuel drum, and short field performance over speed. Seventy years on, they still fly the Alaska routes they were built for.
